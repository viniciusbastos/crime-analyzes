# Main Objective

Crime Analysis turns police incident records into information that supports public security decisions, such as where and when to focus police resources.

The application is built around incident data from the state of Bahia, Brazil. It gives police units and analysts one place to load this data, explore it, and ask questions about it.

## How the Application Supports This Objective

- **Centralize data**: Import incident files into one database, with duplicate detection and reports of rejected rows.
- **Show where crime happens**: Display incidents on heatmaps, cluster maps, and density views.
- **Show when crime happens**: Reveal hourly, weekday, monthly, and semester patterns.
- **Track change over time**: Measure trends and growth rates, produce forecasts, and find incident-free periods.
- **Add social context**: Compare crime with IBGE population and socioeconomic data.
- **Make analysis accessible**: Let users ask questions in English or Portuguese through the AI chat, without writing queries.
- **Fit the police structure**: Filter data by OPM, AISP, and RISP.

## Intended Users

- **Police commanders and units**, who review crime in their area on the web or mobile app.
- **Analysts and administrators**, who upload data, manage users, and maintain the system.
